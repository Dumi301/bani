Bani — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Bani is an on-device expense logger for iPhone. It has no account system and no server of its own for your financial data — everything you record lives in a local database on your device. This policy explains what the optional bank sync feature does with your data.

What Bani stores, and where

  • Your transactions stay in a local database on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us and are not shared with third parties.
  • Bank connection credentials — the worker URL and a device token you enter in Settings — are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are set to device-only access, never sync to iCloud, and are never written to app logs, backups you export, or our code.
  • Bani contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

How bank sync works

Bank sync is off until you enter your credentials. When enabled, Bani talks to a proxy (the "Bani worker") that forwards requests to the open-banking provider Enable Banking. The worker adds the provider's own authentication and relays the provider's responses back to the app unchanged. Connecting a bank opens your bank's own secure consent page; Bani never sees your online-banking username or password.

  • Bani requests read-only access to your account balances and transactions, for the consent window your bank grants.
  • Transactions the provider returns are imported into the local database on your device and categorized there. No copy is kept off-device by Bani.

Disconnecting

"Unlink & wipe keys" in Settings revokes the bank session, deletes the stored connection metadata, and wipes the credentials from your Keychain. Transactions already imported remain on your device until you delete them.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your financial data. The only external service involved in bank sync is the open-banking provider you explicitly connect to, reached through the Bani worker, for the sole purpose of fetching the accounts and transactions you consented to.

Bani is distributed as an unsigned app for personal use. Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's source repository.
